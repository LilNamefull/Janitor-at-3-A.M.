A project undertaken during the second semester of the degree course.


Janitor at 3 A.M. is a 3D-First Person Horror Game where you play a Janitor who takes on a shady way overpaid job at a highschool to pay for your sick wife. 
At day everythings seams normal but at night you have to avoid a Monster roming the halls, while doing so you meet NPCs who will help you understand whats going on and help you survive
